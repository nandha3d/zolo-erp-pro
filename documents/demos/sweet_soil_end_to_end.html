Why no job work challan here? The manufacturer owns the raw material and sells you finished bulk product, so it is a normal purchase. Filling and labelling happen at your own unit, so it is an in-house packing run (BOM), not job work. You would need a job work DC (Sec 143, ITC-04) only if you sent your own material (for example, your printed pouches) to someone else's premises.

Do it yourself in zoloERP

Open the picker at http://127.0.0.1:8080/select-company and choose Phoenicle Natures Private Limited (Demo). The screens below hold the same documents. To run the next cycle, follow the same order.

  1. Masters › Customers / Suppliers: give every customer a channel (customer group) and credit days, and enter the GSTIN (state comes from it). Open
  2. Masters › Items: one item per bulk product and packing material, plus each branded pack (batch-tracked, MRP, HSN, GST). Add a CASE unit (×50) for distributor packs. Open
  3. Operations › Manufacturing › BOM: one packing recipe per pack size (bulk kg + pouch + label, spillage %). Open
  4. Purchases › New bill (F2): the manufacturer's bulk invoice and the packing vendors' invoices, with their invoice numbers. Open
  5. Operations › Manufacturing › Production: plan the run, then complete it with the packed quantity, labour cost, batch no., mfg/expiry date and MRP. Open
  6. Sales › New invoice (F12): pick the customer and the channel price; batches fill in by FEFO; choose a scheme for free goods; IGST applies on its own for out-of-state buyers. Open
  7. Add payment on the invoice / bill: record UPI/NEFT as Bank and cheques as Cheque, with the reference in the note.
  8. Reports: Product expiry · GST report · Trial balance. Then rerun php artisan erp:demo-sweet-soil --report-only to refresh this page.
