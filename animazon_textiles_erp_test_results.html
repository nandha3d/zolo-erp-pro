Textile Supply Chain & Job Work Story Journey Real-World Scenario

Animazon Textiles Pvt Ltd purchases 400 Plain Cloth pieces from MCR Textiles Pvt Ltd. The goods are dispatched to job worker Vijay Dyeing Processors under GST Rule 55 Delivery Challan for Royal Blue coloring. Once processed, the goods are received back converted as Animazon Dyed Premium Fabric (Royal Blue). Finally, 350 units are sold B2B to Vogue Apparels B2B generating substantial gross profit while leaving 50 units in closing inventory.
