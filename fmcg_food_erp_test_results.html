FMCG Food Manufacturing & Multi-Tier Distribution Journey Real-World Scenario

NutriCrunch Foods Pvt Ltd (FSSAI Lic # 12423001000456) manufactures healthy cereal snack bars. The company procures raw ingredients (Rolled Oats, California Almonds, Wild Honey) and printed nitrogen foil packaging. Bulk cooked granola slabs and 6,000 pouches are dispatched to certified co-packer FreshPack Contract Packaging Services under statutory GST Section 143 Delivery Challans for nitrogen flushing, pouch sealing, and batch coding. The finished goods (5,800 pouches of NutriCrunch Granola Bar 40g, Batch LOT-NC-202610-A) are received back with 200 pouches machine scrap allowance. The goods are distributed across three commercial tiers: Tier 1 Super Stockist (FTL bulk), Tier 2 Wholesaler (Master Cartons), and Tier 3 Modern Retailer (Display Caddies). Reverse logistics handles a customer market return with QA inspection (20 restocked, 30 condemned), followed by certified disposal and statutory CGST Section 17(5)(h) ITC Reversal.
